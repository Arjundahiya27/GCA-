Why do leaves need guard cells if they have stomata?
Clear answerOnly from your NCERT textbook

Guard cells are the two cells around each pore. They decide when it opens.

1Water flows in, they swell, the pore opens.
2Water flows out, they shrink, the pore closes.
Stomata are the door. Guard cells hold the key.
